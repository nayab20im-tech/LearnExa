# LearnExa Brand Guide

## Identity

**LearnExa** combines learning, exploration, and excellence. It represents a complete assessment platform rather than only a quiz application.

**Tagline:** Learn · Explore · Excel

## Color tokens

```text

Gold          #FFD66B
Ink           #101D38
Canvas        #F7F9FD
```

## Typography

- Headings: Sora
- Interface and body copy: Manrope

## Logo concept

The mark combines:

- An open book for learning
- A rising check/arrow for achievement
- A small spark for discovery
- Blue and aqua for intelligence and growth
- Coral for energy and progress

## Writing style

LearnExa copy should feel:

- Clear
- Confident
- Encouraging
- Professional
- Student-friendly

Avoid exaggerated marketing language, crowded paragraphs, and unexplained technical terms on public screens.
